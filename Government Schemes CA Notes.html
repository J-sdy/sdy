🏛️ State Government Schemes — 2026

1. Mukhyamantri Sehat Bima Yojana Punjab

  • ✅ Free & cashless healthcare to eligible families
  • 💰 Health coverage: up to ₹10 lakh/family
  • 🔹 Covers 2,200+ medical procedures

2. One District One Cuisine Scheme Uttar Pradesh

  • Promotes the unique cuisine of each district

3. Guru Dakshina Uttar Pradesh

  • Also called CM Cashless Medical Scheme for Teachers
  • Cashless healthcare for teachers, education staff & families
  • 💰 Coverage: up to ₹5 lakh/family/year

4. Lakhpati Beti Yojana Delhi

  • Financial support to girl children, birth → graduation (paid in installments)
  • 💰 Maturity amount: ₹1 lakh
  • ⚠️ Max 2 daughters/family

5. ANMOL Scheme Delhi

  • Full form: Advanced Newborn Monitoring for Optimal Lifecare
  • Free genetic testing for newborns
  • Detects genetic & metabolic disorders early
💡 Common genetic disorders among babies (memorize as "D-S-T"):
  • Down Syndrome → Chromosome 21 → congenital heart disease
  • Sickle Cell Disorder → Hemoglobin disorder → sickle-shaped blood cells
  • Thalassemia → Hemoglobin disorder → spleen, liver & blood issues

6. Delhi Lakshmi Yojana Delhi

  • 💰 ₹2,500/month via DBT to eligible women
  • 👤 Beneficiaries: women aged 21–60 years

7. Fare Se Fursat Scheme Madhya Pradesh

  • Affordable & assured air travel
  • ✈️ First route: Rewa–Raipur
  • Airline: Alliance Air

8. DWEEPTI Scheme Chhattisgarh

  • Promotes rural women entrepreneurship
  • Focus sector: Solar energy
  • Aim: create women entrepreneurs in solar sector
💡 Memory trick: Order = Punjab → UP (×2) → Delhi (×3) → MP → Chhattisgarh. Think "P-U-U-D-D-D-M-C" — Punjab, UP twice, Delhi thrice, MP, Chhattisgarh.

🏢 Central Government Schemes — 2026

1. RELIEF Scheme

  • Full form: Resilience & Logistics Intervention for Export Facilitation
  • Ministry: Commerce & Industry
  • Helps exporters hit by West Asia crisis
  • Credit insurance + freight support, esp. for MSMEs
📅 Launched 19 March 2026 — targeted support for freight escalation, high insurance premia & war-related export risks in Gulf/West Asia corridor. Implemented via ECGC (nodal agency).

2. PRARAMBH 2026 Campaign

  • Ministry: Finance / CBDT
  • Creates awareness on Income Tax Act, 2025
  • Promotes taxpayer awareness

3. Say No to Proxy Sarpanch Campaign

  • Ministry: Panchayati Raj
  • Stops proxy leadership in Panchayats
  • ⚠️ Targets "Sarpanch Pati" practice — male relatives exercising power on behalf of elected women representatives

4. BHAVYA — Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna

  • Ministry: Commerce & Industry, implemented via DPIIT
  • Develops 100 world-class plug-and-play industrial parks
  • Supports industrial infra & manufacturing

5. PM RAHAT

  • Ministry: Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH)
  • Cashless treatment to road accident victims
  • Available up to 7 days post-accident
  • 💰 Up to ₹1.5 lakh/victim

6. Pradhan Mantri Cha (Chai) Shramik Protsahan Yojana (PMCSPY)

  • Ministry: Commerce & Industry / Tea Board of India
  • Supports tea garden workers & families
  • Mainly covers Assam & West Bengal
  • Benefits ~10 lakh workers & families
  • Supports healthcare, education infra, rest shelters
📍 Board HQs: Tea Board → West Bengal | Coffee & Silk Board → Bangalore | Rubber Board → Kottayam

7. Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS) → PLI Scheme

  • 📅 Launched: July 2026
  • Ministry: MeitY · Budget: ₹62,500 Cr · Outlay: 5 years
  • Promotes domestic mobile manufacturing & value addition
  • Supports Indian-owned brands & R&D

8. Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana (PM-SSY)

  • Ministry: New & Renewable Energy (MNRE)
  • Promotes floating solar power projects + Energy Storage Systems
  • Uses reservoirs, dams & inland water bodies

9. Samundra Manthan Scheme

  • Also: National Offshore Exploration Scheme
  • Ministry: Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG)
  • Promotes deepwater & ultra-deepwater oil/gas exploration
  • Aim: increase offshore energy-resource exploration along coast

🏢 Central Government Schemes — 2025

1. PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana

  • Covers 100 low-productivity agri districts
  • Improves productivity, irrigation, crop diversification, post-harvest storage
  • Ministry: Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

2. Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses

  • 📅 Target period: 2025–26 to 2030–31
  • Focus crops: Tur (Arhar), Urad, Masoor
  • Aim: ↑ pulses production, ↓ import dependence
  • Ministry: Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

3. PM-SETU

  • Upgrades 1,000 Govt ITIs into modern training institutes
  • Model: hub-and-spoke — 200 hub ITIs + 800 spoke ITIs
  • Ministry: Skill Development & Entrepreneurship

4. Gyan Bharatam Mission

  • Preservation, conservation & digitisation of Indian manuscripts
  • Ministry: Culture

5. National Mission on High Yielding Seeds

  • Develops high-yielding, pest-resistant, climate-resilient seeds
  • Ministry: Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

6. Mission for Cotton Productivity

  • 5-year mission — climate-resilient, pest-resistant, high-yield cotton (incl. ELS Extra-Long Staple)
  • Nodal: Dept. of Agri Research & Education; partner: Ministry of Textiles

7. National Mission on Edible Oils – Oilseeds (2024)

  • ↑ domestic oilseed production → self-reliance in edible oil
  • Ministry: Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

8. Sintered Rare-Earth Permanent Magnet (REPM) Scheme

  • Domestic manufacturing of rare-earth magnets
  • ↓ import dependence in EVs, renewable energy, electronics, defence
  • Ministry: Heavy Industries

9. PM SVANidhi (Restructured, extended till 2030)

  • Enhanced credit support to street vendors
  • Includes UPI-linked RuPay credit cards
  • Ministry: Housing & Urban Affairs
💡 SVANidhi basics: launched 2020 by MoHUA · collateral-free loans up to ₹50,000 in tranches: ₹10k → ₹20k → ₹50k (per notes: ₹15k/₹25k/₹50k across sources — remember increasing tranche pattern).

10. National Manufacturing Mission (2025–26)

  • Covers small, medium & large industries
  • Strengthens manufacturing under Make in India
  • Ministry: Commerce & Industry (DPIIT)

11. Makhana Board

  • Set up in Bihar — improves makhana production, processing, value addition, marketing
  • Supports farmers & FPOs
  • Ministry: Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
💡 Mnemonic (2025 list order): "Dhan-Pulse-SETU-Gyan, Seed-Cotton-Oil-REPM, SVANidhi-Manufacturing-Makhana" — read as one flowing sentence to recall sequence.

🌾 Viksit Bharat–G RAM G Act, 2025

  • Full form: Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)
  • ⚠️ Replaces MGNREGA, 2005 from 1 July 2026
  • Guaranteed employment: 100 → 125 days/household/year
  • 60 days kept as no-work period during sowing & harvesting seasons
  • Wages paid weekly or within 15 days
  • If work not given in time → unemployment allowance
  • Ministry: Rural Development

Four Main Areas

  • 💧 Water Security — harvesting, irrigation, groundwater recharge
  • 🛣️ Rural Infrastructure — roads & basic infra
  • 💼 Livelihood Infrastructure — local livelihood opportunities
  • 🌦️ Climate Resilience — deals with extreme weather/disasters

Funding

  • Centrally Sponsored Scheme
  • Centre : State = 60 : 40
  • NE & Himalayan States = 90 : 10
  • UTs without legislature = 100% Central funding

Implementation

  • Gram Panchayats — plan & carry out works
  • At least 50% of works (by cost) at Gram Panchayat level
  • Gram Sabhas conduct social audits
  • Biometric authentication, geo-referencing, digital monitoring used for tracking

⚡ Quick Revision Summary

  • State schemes: Punjab(health), UP(cuisine/teachers), Delhi(girl child/newborn/women), MP(air travel), Chhattisgarh(women solar)
  • Central 2026 theme: exports crisis (RELIEF), tax awareness (PRARAMBH), panchayat reform (Proxy Sarpanch), industrial parks (BHAVYA), accident care (PM RAHAT), tea workers (PMCSPY), mobile mfg (MPMS/PLI), floating solar (PM-SSY), offshore oil (Samundra Manthan)
  • Central 2025 theme: agri-productivity missions (Dhan-Dhaanya, Pulses, Seeds, Cotton, Oilseeds), skilling (PM-SETU), heritage (Gyan Bharatam), manufacturing (REPM, Manufacturing Mission), vendors (SVANidhi), Bihar (Makhana Board)
  • VB-G RAM G Act 2025 replaces MGNREGA from 1 Jul 2026: 100→125 days, 60-day no-work period, Centre:State = 60:40 (NE/Himalayan 90:10)
  • Ministry pairing tip: Health(NHM, PM-JAY, eSanjeevani) · Agri&FW(PM-KISAN, PMFBY, KCC, SHC) · Jal Shakti(Namami Gange, Jal Jeevan) · Housing&Urban(Smart Cities, AMRUT, PMAY-U, SVANidhi)
  • Apps vs Portals: Apps = citizen-facing mobile tools (UMANG, DigiLocker, ABHA); Portals = web-based service/monitoring systems (NSWS, e-Shram, Jan Samarth)
  • ⚠️ Common confusion: SVANidhi = MoHUA (urban street vendors) vs DAY-NRLM = Rural Dev (rural women SHGs)
  • ⚠️ PM-KISAN (income support ₹6,000/yr, no age limit) ≠ PM-KMY (pension scheme, age 18–40, ₹3,000/month after 60)
