⚖️ BNS · BNSS · BSA — Visual Notes

Siddhi Prep — GS ASTRA Series | SSC Exams 2026

📘 Introduction

✅ On 1 July 2024 — new criminal laws BNS, BNSS, BSA replaced old colonial laws:
  • IPC (Indian Penal Code) → BNS
  • CrPC (Criminal Procedure Code) → BNSS
  • Indian Evidence Act, 1872 → BSA

🔹 Indian Penal Code (IPC) — Background

  • Introduced by Macaulay in 1860
  • 1st Law Commission established under Charter Act 1833
  • 💡 Earlier: Codification of Gentoo Laws, 1776 (situation before 1860)

🔹 Core Philosophy of IPC — British Utilitarianism

  • Actions right if they promote happiness; wrong if they cause suffering
  • ⚠️ Intentions don't matter — only outcomes matter
  • Impartiality to be maintained while applying laws

⚠️ Issues in IPC (1860)

  • Primary goal = Punishment (old justice system) vs modern goal = Reform
  • New-age offences not covered: Terrorism, Organised Crime, Mob Lynching, Cyber Crime, Digital offences
  • Crimes against women/children scattered across multiple chapters (unorganised)
  • Courts struck down colonial sections post-independence:
    • 🔹 Sec 377 (homosexuality criminalised) → struck in Navtej Singh Johar v. UOI
    • 🔹 Sec 497 (adultery) → struck in Joseph Shine v. UOI
💡 Why replaced? To overhaul laws, consolidate & reorganise offences, add digital-age crimes, and ensure citizen-friendly justice delivery.

🔹 Components of Overhaul (examples)

  • IPC Sec 302 (Culpable homicide → murder) → BNS Sec 101 (verbatim)
  • IPC Sec 375 (Rape) → BNS Sec 63 — now allows digital/electronic evidence recognition

🩸 BNS — Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (replaces IPC)

🔹 The Sedition Debate (old Sec 124A)

Old definition: Words/signs/visible representation bringing hatred/contempt/disaffection against Government → punishable with life imprisonment or up to 3 years + fine.
  • ⚠️ Leaders imprisoned under sedition (historical):
    • Bal Gangadhar Tilak — 1908 (Kesari newspaper)
    • Gandhi — 1922 Gujarat trials (Young India writings)
    • Maulana Abul Kalam Azad — 1922
    • Jogendra Chandra Bose — 1891
  • 💡 Trick: "T-G-A-J" → Tilak, Gandhi, Azad, Jogendra (in order 1891→1908→1922→1922)
  • Sedition was misused → removed in BNS; replaced by Section 152
Sec 152 — Acts endangering sovereignty, unity & integrity of India
Exciting/attempting secession, armed rebellion, subversive activities, separatist feelings, endangering sovereignty/unity/integrity → punishable with life imprisonment or up to 7 years + fine.

⚠️ Criticism: Wording "excites or attempts to excite secession/armed rebellion/subversive activities" still broad enough to suppress dissent.

🔹 Terrorism Defined — Section 113 (new)

  • Terrorist act = any act intending to:
    • Threaten unity, integrity, sovereignty, security or economic security of India, OR
    • Intimidate the general public, OR
    • Disturb public order
  • Punishment: Death penalty or fine ≥ ₹10 Lakh (if act causes death)
  • If death doesn't occur: Imprisonment + fine, both not less than 5 years / ₹5 Lakh
  • ⚠️ Officer not below rank of Superintendent of Police (SP) decides whether to register under Sec 113 (BNS) or invoke UAPA, 1967

🔹 Mob Lynching, Organised & Petty Organised Crime (New Concepts)

  • Organised crime: kidnapping, extortion, cyber-crime committed on behalf of a crime syndicate
  • Mob Lynching: murder by 5+ persons on grounds of caste, language, personal belief etc. → Punishment: 7 years to life imprisonment or death
  • Petty Organised Crime (Sec 112): vehicle theft, house/business/cargo theft, snatching & organised pickpocketing, shoplifting/card skimming, ATM thefts, cheating & trick theft
    • Cognizable (no warrant), No bail, Prison 1–7 years

🔹 Crimes Against Women & Children

  • All such crimes now organised into a single chapter
  • Age to classify rape victim as major: 16 → 18 years
  • Sexual intercourse via false promise (marriage/job/fake identity) = offence
  • Free first aid/medical treatment guaranteed to victims at all hospitals
  • Statement of victim (delicate offences) recorded by woman Magistrate; in her absence, male Magistrate + woman present
  • 'Beggary' introduced as a form of exploitation for trafficking — punishable under Sec 143
  • Theft now includes digital theft

🔹 Community Service (New Punishment)

  • For minor offences only
  • To be ordered by the court

🔹 Unsound Mind → Mental Illness

BNS replaces 'unsound mind' with 'mental illness' as defined in Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 — a substantial disorder of thinking/orientation/memory that grossly impairs capacity to recognise reality.
⚠️ Excludes mental retardation/incomplete mind development.

🔹 Minimum Age of Criminal Responsibility

  • Below 7 years — no offence (retained from IPC)
  • 7–12 years — offence only if child lacks maturity to understand nature/consequences
  • ⚠️ Higher than several jurisdictions; UN Committee (2007) recommended raising age above 12 years

🚓 BNSS — Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (replaces CrPC)

  • CrPC: first enacted 1861, replaced in 1974 — procedure for investigation, arrest, search & seizure, evidence collection, trials
  • Cognizable offence: serious crime, arrest without warrant, FIR mandatory
  • Compoundable offence: legal compromise / out-of-court settlement allowed
  • 💡 Reason for replacement: improvise investigation with modern tech + speedy justice

🔹 Key Improvisations

  • 1️⃣ Forensics in investigation
  • 2️⃣ E-Trial — all trials/inquiries/proceedings may be held in electronic mode; production of electronic devices likely containing digital evidence needs digital signature
  • 3️⃣ Detention procedures
  • 4️⃣ Bail procedures
  • 5️⃣ Timelines for procedures
  • 6️⃣ Zero FIR
  • 7️⃣ Power to attach properties — movable & immovable

🔹 Zero FIR — Section 173 BNSS

✅ For cognizable offences only
➡️ Can be filed at any police station (regardless of jurisdiction)
➡️ FIR copy marked with "0"; Investigating Officer conducts preliminary investigation & forwards to concerned P.S.
➡️ Concerned P.S. re-registers the case

🔹 Portal: Digital Police Portal

🔹 CCTNS

Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems (CCTNS) — nationwide project under Ministry of Home Affairs + NCRB. Connects police stations & higher offices → centralised database of crime/criminal records. ✅ Used for accessing FIRs digitally.

🔹 Custody & Detention (High-Yield for Exams)

  • ⚠️ Max police custody = 15 days total (even if sought in parts) — must be within initial remand stage
  • Total detention (investigation) cannot exceed:
    • Ordinary offences (punishment < 10 yrs): 60 days → default bail after
    • Serious offences (punishment ≥ 10 yrs / life / death): 90 days → default bail after
  • 💡 Trick: 15 (custody) is inside 60/90 (total detention) — doesn't extend total time
  • Protects Article 21 — right to personal liberty

🔹 Search & Seizure — Section 105 BNSS

Every search/seizure must be audio-video recorded, preferably via mobile phone — includes list of seized items + witnesses' signatures on video. Recording sent promptly to District/Sub-divisional/1st Class Judicial Magistrate. ✅ Ensures transparency & prevents misuse of power.

🔹 Witness Protection — Section 398

  • Every state must create a Witness Protection Scheme (risk-based levels)
  • Allows video conferencing for statements/court attendance
  • Identity concealment for vulnerable witnesses
  • ⚠️ NOT included: anonymous juries, SMS-based statements

🔍 BSA — Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (replaces Indian Evidence Act, 1872)

  • Scope: all judicial proceedings including court martials
  • Key change areas: Digital evidence, Oral evidence, Secondary evidence, Evidence before police officer, Expert advice

🔹 Digital Evidence

  • Emails, server logs, smartphone messages, location data, video files, blockchain records → all considered "Document" (digital evidence)
  • ⚠️ Under old IPC/Evidence Act: electronic record = secondary evidence
  • ✅ Under BSA: electronic record = primary evidence
  • Admissible only with a Technical Certificate (Section 65B)
Technical Certificate — 2 Parts:
  • Part A: filled by person producing record — states it was under their lawful possession
  • Part B: certified by technical expert
  • Must give metadata + digital hash value

🔹 Direct Oral Evidence — Section 55

  • Must be direct, first-hand
  • ⚠️ Explicitly excludes hearsay and assumptions

🔹 Confessions Before Police Officer — Section 23

  • ⚠️ Generally inadmissible in court
  • ✅ Exception: admissible if confession leads to recovery of an artifact
  • Only voluntary confessions before a Magistrate are admissible

🔹 Expert Opinion

  • Forensic analysts, medical officers etc. — opinion is only advisory & persuasive, not binding

🔹 Secondary Evidence — Section 60

  • Rule: documents ordinarily proved by primary evidence (original)
  • Secondary evidence (photocopies/scans) admissible only if:
    • Original lost/destroyed
    • Held by opposite party / beyond court's reach
    • Certified copies of public documents produced

✅ Quick Revision Summary

  • 1 July 2024 — BNS, BNSS, BSA came into force, replacing IPC, CrPC, Indian Evidence Act (1872) respectively
  • BNS = Offences (Sedition removed → Sec 152; Terrorism → Sec 113; Mob Lynching, Organised/Petty Organised crime new; Crimes vs women/children in single chapter; age of rape victim 16→18; Community service for minor offences)
  • BNSS = Procedure (Police custody max 15 days; total detention 60 days/90 days; Zero FIR Sec 173; e-FIR via Digital Police Portal; CCTNS for FIR access; Search/Seizure video recording Sec 105; Witness Protection Sec 398)
  • BSA = Evidence (Digital records = primary evidence now; Technical Certificate Sec 65B (Part A + B); Oral evidence must be direct Sec 55; Police confessions inadmissible Sec 23 (except artifact recovery); Secondary evidence only in exceptions Sec 60; Expert opinion advisory only)
  • ⚠️ Common trap: BSA doesn't have a "Section 62=secondary" trick — Sec 62 defines primary evidence; certificate requirement is Sec 65B
  • 💡 28/28 PYQs in this set are from Gs → Polity → Miscellaneous — very high exam weightage topic

🔹 Fast recall trick: "15 goes into 60, 60 goes into 90" — custody(15) ⊂ ordinary detention(60) ⊂ serious detention(90)
