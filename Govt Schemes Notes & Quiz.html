🌾 Fertilizer Sector Overview
⚗️
Fertilizer Structure & N:P:K Imbalance
Ideal N:P:K Ratio4:2:1
India's Actual Ratio8:3:1 or higher (urea overuse)
N – NitrogenUrea
P – PhosphorusDAP – Di-Ammonium Phosphate
K – PotassiumMOP – Muriate of Potash
⚠️ Import Dependence: Urea–partial | Phosphatic–heavy | Potassic–almost entirely imported
🧪
Urea Subsidy & Neem Coated Urea
  • Urea sold at govt-fixed MRP; difference paid via DBT to companies
  • Artificially cheap → over-application of nitrogen → imbalanced N:P:K
🌿 Neem Coated Urea: Neem = natural nitrification inhibitor → slow nutrient release → better absorption → curbs diversion/black marketing. 100% mandatory for all urea production
⚖️
NBS Scheme
Nutrient Based Subsidy – P&K Fertilizers
  • Govt fixes per kg subsidy rate for each nutrient (N,P,K,S)
  • Companies set own prices (market-based)
  • Result: Urea cheap+overused vs P&K costlier+underused
🇮🇳
One Nation One Fertilizer
Brand: "Bharat"
  • All subsidized fertilizers under brand "Bharat"
  • "Bharat Urea", "Bharat DAP", "Bharat NPK"
  • Reduces farmer confusion
🔬
Nano Fertilizer Initiatives
  • Nanomaterials <100 nm for controlled nutrient release
  • Up to 95% nutrient uptake
  • Boost yields by 20–22%, reduces wastage & leaching

Q 1Direct FactEasy
Under PM-KISAN, what is the annual income support and disbursement mode for eligible farmer families?
  • A)₹4,000 in 2 installments via state govt
  • B)₹6,000 in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each via DBT
  • C)₹8,000 in 4 installments via cooperative banks
  • D)₹6,000 in 2 installments of ₹3,000 each
✅ Correct: B) ₹6,000 in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each via DBT
Explanation: PM-KISAN (Feb 2019) is a Central Sector Scheme providing ₹6,000/year in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 each via Direct Benefit Transfer. It is 100% funded by the Central Government. Income-tax payers, doctors, engineers, lawyers and constitutional post-holders are excluded.
Why others are wrong:
  • A — Amount wrong (₹4,000) and state govt mode is wrong; PM-KISAN uses DBT from Centre.
  • C — ₹8,000 and cooperative banks are both incorrect.
  • D — The split is 3 installments of ₹2,000, not 2 installments of ₹3,000.
Q 2Conceptual TrapMedium
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme. What does this mean about its funding pattern?
  • A)60:40 Centre:State ratio for general states
  • B)75:25 Centre:State ratio
  • C)100% funded by Government of India
  • D)50:50 Centre:State partnership
✅ Correct: C) 100% funded by Government of India
Explanation: Central Sector Schemes are 100% centrally funded — states have zero financial contribution. This is the critical distinction from Centrally Sponsored Schemes (like PMFBY) that have Centre-State cost-sharing. PM-KISAN being a Central Sector scheme means no state can delay or reduce disbursements due to fund mismatch.
Why others are wrong:
  • A — 60:40 is the pattern for Centrally Sponsored Schemes like MGNREGS; not PM-KISAN.
  • B — 75:25 is used for some CSS in NE states; not applicable to PM-KISAN.
  • D — PM-KISAN has zero state funding contribution.

Which of the following is EXCLUDED from PM-KISAN benefits?
  • A)A marginal farmer owning 1 hectare in a tribal area
  • B)A woman farmer registered as landowner
  • C)A farmer who is also a retired government employee drawing pension > ₹10,000/month
  • D)A farmer whose son is a private company employee
✅ Correct: C) A farmer who is also a retired government employee drawing pension > ₹10,000/month
Explanation: PM-KISAN excludes: income tax payees, institutional landholders, constitutional post-holders, serving/retired government employees drawing ≥₹10,000/month pension, and professionals like doctors/engineers/lawyers/CAs. Retired govt employees drawing substantial pension are excluded, but mere private employment of a family member does not disqualify the farmer.
Why others are wrong:
  • A — Tribal/marginal farmers with small landholdings are the primary target; eligible.
  • B — Women landowners are fully eligible and encouraged to register.
  • D — A family member's private employment doesn't disqualify the farmer beneficiary.
Q 4Factual TwistMedium
Under PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY), which institution manages the pension fund?
  • A)NABARD — ₹5,000/month pension after age 60

✅ Correct: B) LIC — ₹3,000/month pension after age 60

A farmer insures his banana crop (a horticultural crop) under PMFBY. What premium percentage will he pay?

The Soil Health Card tests soil once in how many years, and tests how many parameters?

Q 13ConceptualMedium
A farmer repays his KCC crop loan promptly. What is the effective interest rate he pays?
  • A)7% per annum (base rate only)
  • B)4% per annum (after prompt repayment incentive)
  • C)5% per annum
  • D)3% per annum
✅ Correct: B) 4% per annum (after prompt repayment incentive)
